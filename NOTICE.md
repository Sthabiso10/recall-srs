## ts-fsrs

<https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/ts-fsrs>

Used in `@recall-srs/core` for:

- the FSRS-6 default weights and per-parameter bounds
  (`src/algorithm/fsrs-params.ts`)
- the 17 → 21 and 19 → 21 weight migration
  (`migrateWeights`, ported from `migrateParameters`)
- the banded interval fuzz (`src/algorithm/fuzz.ts`, ported from
  `get_fuzz_range` / `apply_fuzz`)
- the conformance fixture in `packages/core/test/fixtures/ts-fsrs-reference.json`,
  generated by running ts-fsrs
